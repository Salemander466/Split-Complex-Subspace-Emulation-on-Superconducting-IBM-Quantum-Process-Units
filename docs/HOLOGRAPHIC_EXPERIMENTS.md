# Holographic Analogy Experiments

These experiments use spacetime terminology as an analogy for reversible scrambling, inverse recovery, boundary disturbance, and conditional feedback in ordinary Hilbert-space quantum circuits. They do not claim physical wormholes, closed timelike curves, gravitational dynamics, or non-standard quantum mechanics.
